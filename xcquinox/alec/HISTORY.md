# xcquinox/alec -- development & improvement history

This file is the canonical record of every methodology- or results-affecting change to the
`xcquinox/alec/` research subpackage, and the source for the paper's methods/development narrative.

**Logging rule (repo convention):** every such change -- features; physics / loss / descriptor /
training-scheme changes; data, benchmark, or reference-value changes; basis / grid / density-fitting
changes; and any results-changing bug fix -- gets an entry here in the *same* change, recording the date,
commit short-hash, WHAT changed, and **Why:** the rationale. Keep the commit-message body consistent with
the entry. Pure scaffolding / formatting / test-only commits may be grouped into one line or omitted.

Dates are authoring dates; hashes are short git hashes (verifiable via `git show <hash>`).

## Phase 3 -- PBE anchoring, integration-weighted pretraining, Round-2/3 physics review (2026-04-18 to 2026-04-28)

- 2026-04-18 `8f38a7997`/`d85d9c773` -- integration-weighted pretrain MSE via `PretrainSpec.loss_weighting` (default integration). **Why:** fitting F_x should minimize the *integrated* XC-energy residual, not a per-sample mean.
- 2026-04-21 `9de4739a3`/`ce606b1e1`/`857a6e27c` -- `PBEAnchorSample` + `pbe_anchor_loss` (UKS-aware, libxc targets, spin-scaled) + `pbe_anchor_weight` on all losses. **Why:** pin F_x toward the PBE shape so energy-only losses don't drift the large-s asymptote arbitrarily.
- 2026-04-21 (step-6 notebook, ~25 commits) -- Chakravorty atomic-energy dict (`4f6e0b8d8`), H2O/C2H2 W4-11-geometry CCSD + hardened OEP cells, a 72-spec subprocess-isolated training loop, transfer plots, F_x drift panels. **Why:** scale to a 3-group x multi-loss x multi-solver sweep on real W4-11 geometries with held-out transfer molecules.
- 2026-04-27 `533d7c45b` -- replaced the broken softmax channel-gate with real multi-head self-attention. **Why:** the "gate" was not attending; the `_attn` archs needed genuine per-grid attention.
- 2026-04-27 `27080e4a2` (major physics fix) -- corrected three review-caught errors: (1) `idempotency_error` was wrong by a factor of 2 for closed-shell RKS DMs (`DSD=2D`, Szabo-Ostlund 3.144), making the "correlation indicator" near-constant; fixed to the spin-orbital projector form (D'=D/2 RKS, per-spin UKS, Pople-Nesbet 1954). (2) `NonNegativeCorrelation` had an asymptotic floor `1-log2 ~= 0.307` instead of `F_c>=0` (Levy-Perdew); fixed to `softplus((F_raw-1)+log(e-1))`. (3) `CuspDescriptor` docstring mislabeled column 1; corrected to `tanh(log(sum Z_A/r_A)/5)`. **Why:** every model trained against the broken idempotency feature learned from a corrupted descriptor.
- 2026-04-27 `74ba6dde8` -- integration weights now include the Becke quadrature `dr_i`; UKS PBE pretrain targets spin-resolved (libxc `spin=1`); `pbe_anchor` falls back to analytic PBE F_x(s) at rho->0. **Why:** the prior integration mode minimized a weighted *mean* per-sample residual; on atomic Becke grids the weight varies ~6 decades, so omitting it de-calibrated the loss.
- 2026-04-27 `2a3f664ba` -- RKS DM rebuild switched to occupation-mask form `2*(C*occ)@C.T` for gradient stability under degenerate eigenvalues (C2H2/HCN/C2H4 pi-MOs); low-rho JVP guard; `MIXER_REGISTRY`; rejected the incoherent `(FULL, FROZEN)` config. **Why:** reverse-mode through eigh at degenerate shells produced spurious huge gradients.
- 2026-04-27 `6b916d5b5` -- OEP rewrite to the Wu-Yang JCP 118 (2003) displacement form `V_xc = V_xc^baseline + sum_t b_t g_t` with V-space Tikhonov regularization (Heaton-Burgess PRL 98, 2007) and full provenance in the npz. **Why:** start from a physical KS density (b=0=baseline), not a Hartree-only pathology; basis-independent regularization; downstream loaders can validate.
- 2026-04-27 `be7e7d84a` -- fixed three sites passing the spin-summed total DM into `compute_dm_features` for UKS molecules (forcing the RKS branch); fixed UKS one-shot DM to occupation-mask form; JIT-tracer guard. **Why:** the summed DM does not satisfy `DSD=2D`, so every UKS molecule produced a meaningless `idempotency_error`.
- 2026-04-27 `b11c06d47` -- `_dm_term` normalizes by element count (n_ao^2 RKS, 2*n_ao^2 UKS) for an RKS<->UKS-invariant per-element MSE; uniform `molecules_only` gate. **Why:** the UKS branch was off by a factor of 2 vs RKS.
- 2026-04-28 `38332eccd` -- moved `JAX_PLATFORMS=cpu`/`JAX_ENABLE_X64=1` setdefault to conftest module-load time. **Why:** the session-fixture form ran *after* jax initialized its backend, so the suite silently ran on GPU and "hung" thrashing cuSolver.
- 2026-04-28 `28560fe4e` -- closed a round-3 review (OEP Hermitian symmetrization, criterion registry, single-source SCF constants, mixer-state-frozen-after-convergence). **Why:** consolidate findings; single source of truth for SCF constants.
- 2026-04-28 `6261f8ded` -- `_dm_term` uses `math.prod(shape)` not `int(jnp.prod(...))`. **Why:** the jnp form returns a 0-d tracer and `int(tracer)` raises `ConcretizationTypeError` inside the jit step.
- 2026-04-28 `80814a00d` -- enabled `jax_enable_x64=True` in the `_train_one_spec` subprocess. **Why:** it was the only entry point silently inheriting float32; losses below 1e-7 lose precision in fp32, capping convergence.
- 2026-04-28 `a9c251ad4` -- bumped step counts (pretrain 200->1000, short 45->100, long 125->250); added the `L5_gradnorm_vxc` spec (B_atomization_plus_dm + dm_weight=0.1, vxc_weight=0.01, GradNorm alpha=1.5). **Why:** the short budget couldn't move the V_xc residual; GradNorm (Chen et al. 2018, ICML) tracks per-task gradient magnitudes to keep V_xc on par with the fast-falling AE; alpha=1.5 is the paper's robust value.
- 2026-04-28 `f893e3058` -- verified Lieb-Oxford enforcement: 0/90 specs violate F_x > 1.804; the ceiling is `eqx.field(static=True)`. **Why:** confirmed the LOB is architectural (`_AlecLOB`: `1.804*sigmoid(x-log0.804)`); apparent under-saturation is the F_x(s->inf) asymptote, which the LOB does not pin.

## Phase 11 -- DFS SCF-trajectory tail loss + step-decaying mixer (2026-06-24)

- 2026-06-24 `<pending>` (full_25 collapse: root cause + DFS self-consistency fix) -- the `dfs_step7.svp_grid2_v3_full25` run (25-cycle FULL SCF) was catastrophically WORSE than `svp_grid2_v3` (3-cycle): held-out reaction MAE **75-110 kcal/mol vs 13-19**, i.e. 5-7x worse than the PBE baseline (~15), uniformly across all 18 matched specs. **Root cause:** training (`losses._compute_energies`) and eval (`eval_holdout.evaluate_holdout`) both scored ONLY the final SCF cycle's energy (`run_scf().total_energy`). For hard species (transition states, radicals) the NN-driven SCF, given 25 cycles, left the stable PBE-initialized basin and the constant linear mixer (alpha=0.5) could not damp it -- the captured per-cycle traces show a clean **period-2 oscillation** in the tail (e.g. `t-hooo` steps 18-24 alternate -223.40/-223.75/-223.32/-223.72/-223.31/-223.71/-223.30) or are still mid-drift (`s4-c2v` ends 10.3 Ha ~ 6500 kcal/mol off PBE). The final-step energy is then an arbitrary oscillation phase, poisoning both eval (+/-1000-1500 kcal/mol reaction outliers) and training (full25 final loss `8e-3` vs v3 `3.8e-8`, val MAE `212` vs `11.9`, no early-stop). Splitting held-out reactions by SCF convergence proves the mechanism: **converged** reactions MAE ~12-28 (~ v3); **non-converged** reactions MAE 140-485, and there are 26-59 non-converged species per spec. full_3 escapes only because 3 cycles is too few to leave the PBE basin (near-one-shot, stable but inert).

  **The fix (DFS / Dick & Fernandez-Serra 2021, verified verbatim in the pinned `og_dpyscf/og_dpyscf/ogdpyscf/`):** dpyscf forces many SCF cycles but (1) scores only the convergence TAIL with a quadratic weight rising toward convergence (`losses.py:14` `weights = linspace(0,1,N)**2`, `:18` `dE = dE[skip_steps:]`, `train.py:283` `skip_steps = max(5, scf_steps-10)` -> last 10 of 25; energy-only, `rho_loss` uses the final dm), and (2) uses a step-decaying mixer (`torch_routines.py:174-178` `alpha = 0.3**step + 0.3`). We port both:
  - **`solver.DecayingLinearMixer`** (registry `decaying_linear`): `alpha = base**step + floor` (DFS base=floor=0.3 -> step0=1.3 over-relaxation, settling to 0.3), reading the existing `MixerState.step_index` (frozen on convergence).
